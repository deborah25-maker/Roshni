A LITTLE PRAYER FOR YOU ✝️

“May the Lord bless you and keep you; may the Lord make his face shine on you and be gracious to you; may the Lord turn his face toward you and give you peace.”

— Numbers 6:24–26

Roshni,

There are some people God places in our lives who become a blessing without even realizing how much they mean to us.

You are one of those people in my life.

I may not always say everything that is in my heart, and sometimes I may not show it enough, but I hope you always know how grateful I am that God gave me you as my sister.

As you step into another year of your life, my prayer is that you never lose the beautiful heart that makes you who you are.

May God open doors for you that you never expected, give you courage for every new step, and surround you with people who truly value and respect you.

When you have difficult days, I hope you remember that you never have to face life feeling alone. There will always be a place in my heart for you, no matter how far apart we are.

I hope this new year of your life brings you quiet peace, genuine happiness, beautiful opportunities, and dreams that slowly become reality.

Most of all, I pray that when you look back on this year, you will be able to say, “God was with me through it all.”

Thank you for being my sister. Thank you for being someone I can call family. And thank you simply for being you.

Roshni, you are not only a chapter in my childhood.

You are part of my whole story.

No matter how much life changes, you will always be my sister, my family, and someone I will always love.

Happy 26th Birthday, Roshni. ❤️

I love you more than I probably say.

With all my love,
